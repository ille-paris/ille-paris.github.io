Indian Languages and Literature Enthusiasts in Paris (*ille_paris*) is a community of Indian expats and travelers in Paris that celebrated India, its languages, and its literature. We maintain a de-centralized inventory of Indian books in Paris, which co-ordinates the books of our member's individual and personal bookshelves, and can be borrowed and exchanged during our signature silent reading and book exchange events. We organize book clubs, book recitals, events for creative writing, and discourses between Indian authors and Indian readers. And we are soon starting a multi-lingual magazine to publish the writings of the Indian diaspora in Indian languages.

Our participants come from all over India, from all age-groups, and from all walks of life. Just to give an example, at our usual reading events, you might find a mathematician reading a book on ancient Indian counting systems in Marathi, a software developer reading a book on the famine of 1899-90 in Gujarati, a data scientist reading a collection of folk-stories in Malayalam, a biologist reading short-stories in Hindi, a linguist reading essays about Darjeeling's urbanization is Bengali, a mechanical engineer reading poems in Urdu. all seated around the same reading table, and towards the end of the event, having discussions in english, the common language known to all of them.

Ille_paris was founded in early 2024 by Himadri Chhaya-Shailesh, a PhD scholar, as a personal endeavour of proactively keeping in touch with her mother tongue - Gujarati. The early gatherings at the French National Library, sincerely executed a simple idea:
1. Show up to the reading room on time, find yourself a seat, and introduce yourself to the group. 
2. Bring your own book, borrow a book from ille_paris' collection, or from the library, such that the book satisfies at least one of the following criteria:
    - The book is written in one of the Indian languages.
    - The book is a translation from one of the Indian languages.
    - The book is about India.
    - The book is written by an Indian / Indian origin author.
4. Read for a full hour in silence.
5. Discuss about your impression of the book, and decide if you would like to continue reading it, or not!

In February 2025, as a generous reciprocation of ille_paris' humble invitation to come and read with us, the Indian Embassador to France, welcomed us to the Indian embassy's library. And thanks to the enthusiastic support of the Embassy's Press, Information & Culture Counsellor, ille_paris entered a formal collaboration with the Embassy, and now operates from the Embassy's library.
