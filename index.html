About ILLE Paris

Less an institution,
more a living network.

ILLE Paris is a community shaped by a shared affection for Indian literature and the many languages, histories, and lived experiences it carries.

Our vision is to cultivate a decentralised collection of Indian books across Paris — one that lives in homes, cafés, cultural spaces, and embassies rather than behind a single door.

Whether you arrive with a well-worn book or simply an open mind, we warmly welcome anyone curious about Indian literature.
